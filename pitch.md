# The Iron Yard — App Pitch

*A simple, fast, no-monthly-headache app to run your gym.*

---

## The Problem Today

Right now, member details, payments, and attendance are probably scattered across
registers, WhatsApp messages, and memory. That means:

- No easy way to know who's expiring this week
- No clean record of who paid what, when
- No idea how many people actually showed up today
- No professional way to share workout/diet plans with members

**The Iron Yard app fixes all of this — from one phone or tablet at the front desk.**

---

## What the App Does

### For You (Owner/Staff)
- **See everything at a glance:** today's attendance, active members, memberships expiring
  soon, pending payments — all on one home screen
- **Add and manage members** in seconds — search, filter, edit, deactivate
- **Scan attendance with QR** — member shows their digital card, you scan, done. No
  manual registers.
- **Record payments** (cash, bank transfer, UPI/external) with receipts and notes — always
  know who's paid and who hasn't
- **Assign workout & diet plans** to members from reusable templates — build once, assign
  to many
- **Manage trainers and class schedules** — create classes, set capacity, let members book
- **Send announcements** instantly to all members
- **Export reports** — member lists, payment history — anytime you need them for your own
  records or accounting

### For Your Members
- **Digital membership card** with QR code — always in their pocket, nothing to lose
- **See their own status** — expiry date, plans, attendance, payment history — no more
  "when does my membership end?" questions at the counter
- **View workout & diet plans** anytime, even without internet
- **Book classes** directly from the app
- **Get reminders** — before membership expires, before payment is due, before a class
  starts
- **Submit feedback** directly to you instead of forgetting to mention it in person

---

## The Big Advantage: It Works Without Internet

Most gym apps constantly need internet and slow down or fail on bad Wi-Fi. This app is
built **offline-first**:

- Staff can scan attendance even if the internet is down — it saves on the device and
  syncs automatically once connected
- Members can view their card, plans, and history with zero signal
- The app only talks to the server when it actually needs to (login, syncing new data,
  manual refresh) — not on every tap

**Why this matters for you:** faster app, works in gyms with weak Wi-Fi/basement locations,
and lower running costs since we're not making constant server calls.

## New Idea: Daily "Did You Go to the Gym?" Reminder

Once a day, the app can gently ask members: *"Did you go to the gym today?"*

- If yes, they tap Yes, enter roughly what time they went (e.g. 6:00 PM – 7:30 PM), and
  it's logged as their visit
- This works as a **backup attendance log** — useful for members who forget to get
  scanned, or for tracking home/off-hours workouts if that's ever relevant to you
- It also **keeps members engaged with the app daily**, which means they see your
  announcements, plans, and class schedule more often

This costs nothing extra to build or run — it uses the same free local-notification system
as membership and class reminders. We'd treat staff-scanned QR attendance as the official
record, and this as a supporting/engagement feature — happy to adjust based on how you'd
like it to count.

## What's NOT in Version 1 (and why that's a good thing)

- **No online payment gateway yet** — avoids transaction fees and complexity. You keep
  recording payments manually (cash/bank/UPI), which is simpler and cheaper to start.
- **No heavy push-notification infrastructure** — reminders use the phone's own
  notification system, which is free and reliable, instead of a costly always-on push
  service.

This keeps your running costs low while giving you everything you need to manage the gym
day to day. Both can be added later if you ever want them.
